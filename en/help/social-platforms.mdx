## 🎥 Vertical Short-Form Video

Best for 9:16 vertical videos, including Shorts, Reels, and other short-form formats.

| Rank | Platform | Organic Reach Potential | Platform Characteristics and Content Tips |
|:---:|:---|:---:|:---|
| 1 | **TikTok** | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | **Strongest global organic discovery platform**<br/>• Key metrics: first 3-second retention, completion rate, engagement rate<br/>• Recommended length: 10-20 seconds<br/>• Content tips: open with a strong hook, use 9:16, avoid watermarks |
| 2 | **Douyin** | ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆ | **Leading short-video platform in China**<br/>• Distribution: small initial audience test, then expanded based on performance<br/>• Recommended length: 15-30 seconds<br/>• Content tips: fast pacing, recurring series, consistent creator identity |
| 3 | **Kwai** | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | **Combines social connections and algorithmic recommendations**<br/>• Mid-sized accounts can still gain stable organic reach<br/>• Recommended length: flexible and often suitable for longer clips<br/>• Content tips: authenticity, storytelling, recurring formats |
| 4 | **WeChat Channels** | ⭐⭐⭐☆ | **Short video within the WeChat ecosystem**<br/>• Benefits from WeChat's social and community connections<br/>• Recommended length: flexible<br/>• Content tips: add subtitles, focus on a clear niche, connect content with community operations |
| 5 | **Instagram Reels** | ⭐⭐⭐ | **Instagram's short-video format**<br/>• Usually reaches more users organically than standard feed posts<br/>• Recommended length: 5-20 seconds<br/>• Content tips: strong opening, subtitles or stickers, polished visuals |
| 6 | **YouTube Shorts** | ⭐⭐⭐ | **YouTube's short-form video format**<br/>• Slower initial growth but strong long-tail discovery<br/>• Recommended length: up to 60 seconds<br/>• Content tips: optimize for search and connect Shorts with long-form videos |
| 7 | **Facebook Reels** | ⭐⭐☆ | **Facebook's short-video format**<br/>• The algorithm favors vertical short videos<br/>• Recommended length: flexible<br/>• Content tips: paid promotion can extend reach |

<Tip>
**Reference data:** Average organic reach is approximately 3.5% on Instagram and 1-2% on Facebook. Short-form video usually reaches more users than image and text posts.
</Tip>

## 🖼️ Image and Text Content

Best for visually engaging posts that combine images and copy.

| Rank | Platform | Organic Reach Potential | Platform Characteristics and Content Tips |
|:---:|:---|:---:|:---|
| 1 | **RedNote** | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | **Lifestyle discovery community**<br/>• Discovery: recommendations + search<br/>• Content format: multi-image posts (1-9 images)<br/>• Content tips: keyword optimization, guides and checklists, eye-catching covers |
| 2 | **Pinterest** | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | **Visual discovery engine**<br/>• Strong long-tail traffic and a long content lifecycle<br/>• Content format: 2:3 portrait images<br/>• Content tips: keyword-rich descriptions, multiple themed boards, consistent topics |
| 3 | **Instagram** | ⭐⭐⭐ | **Photo Social Leader**<br/>• Feed posts + Reels dual format<br/>• Content Format: Single/multiple images/carousels<br/>• Content Tips: Visual aesthetics, hashtags, engagement rate |
| 4 | **Facebook** | ⭐⭐⭐ | **World's Largest Social Network**<br/>• Supports text/image/video multiple formats<br/>• Content Format: Posts + Reels + Stories<br/>• Content Tips: Community management, ad integration, multi-format testing |
| 5 | **LinkedIn** | ⭐⭐⭐ | **Professional network**<br/>• Personal profiles usually outperform company Pages<br/>• Content format: long-form posts + document carousels<br/>• Content tips: professional insights, industry perspectives, fewer external links |
| 6 | **Threads** | ⭐⭐☆ | **Meta's text-focused social platform**<br/>• Linked to Instagram accounts<br/>• Content format: short text + images (1-4 images)<br/>• Content tips: ask questions and join active conversations |
| 7 | **X/Twitter** | ⭐⭐☆ | **Real-time public conversation platform**<br/>• Fast distribution and strong sensitivity to trends<br/>• Content format: 280 characters + images or video<br/>• Content tips: distinctive voice, timely topics, longer threads |

<Note>
**LinkedIn content tip:** Company Pages generally have lower organic reach. Document and image carousels often perform better than external links or videos, while personal profiles usually reach more users than company Pages.
</Note>

## 📊 Content Strategy Recommendations

### Recommended Content Mix

Based on platform characteristics, consider this content mix:
- 🎥 Short-form video: 60%
- 🖼️ Image and text content: 20%
- 📺 Long-form video: 15%
- 📝 Long-form articles: 5%

### Create Once, Distribute Across Platforms

**Core content:**
1. Create a 9:16 vertical short video lasting 15-30 seconds.
2. Add embedded subtitles and open with a strong hook.
3. Keep the content useful and information-dense.

**Distribution strategy:**
- **Short-video platforms:** Publish directly to Douyin, Kwai, WeChat Channels, TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, and YouTube Shorts.
- **Image and text platforms:** Extract key video frames, add written highlights, and publish to RedNote, Pinterest, LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, X/Twitter, and Threads.
- **Long-form content:** Turn in-depth material into an article for WeChat Official Accounts.
