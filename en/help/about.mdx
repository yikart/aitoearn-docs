Expanding content overseas is an important goal for many Chinese creators and businesses. This guide explains how to register and use accounts on major international social media platforms safely and consistently.

## Meta Platform Accounts

### Platform Overview

Meta platforms, including Facebook, Instagram, Threads, and WhatsApp, have extremely large daily active user bases overseas and are important channels for global content distribution.

### Registration Challenges

**Meta accounts are currently among the most difficult platform accounts to register.** Reviews for users in mainland China are particularly strict, and the risk of account suspension is high.

### Common Approach

Because registration can be difficult, a common approach among Chinese users is:
- **Purchase an existing account and update its personal information.**
- Prioritize a Facebook account, because Meta products generally require or recommend signing in with Facebook.

## Other Major International Platforms

### Supported Platforms

Compared with Meta platforms, the following platforms are much easier to register for directly:

- **TikTok** 
- **YouTube** 
- **Twitter (X)** 
- **Pinterest** 
- **LinkedIn** 

### Registration Preparation

#### 1. Get an Overseas Phone Number

**Recommended option:** Purchase a UK SIM card on Taobao for about RMB 20.

**Steps:**
1. Activate the SIM card after receiving it.
2. Add credit to the card. A single top-up can keep it active for an extended period.
3. Use it to receive verification codes from each platform.

#### 2. Choose a Suitable Registration Environment

- Use the **Aitoearn cloud browser environment** (recommended).
- Or use another clean network environment that has not been associated with risky accounts.

### Account Usage Considerations

#### Environment Consistency
- Keep the sign-in environment consistent whenever possible.
- Avoid frequently changing IP addresses or devices.
- Keep the environment clean to reduce account risk.
- Platforms can identify devices and IP addresses. If a new account uses the same device, IP address, or browser as a suspended account, changing the email address or phone number may not prevent the accounts from being linked.

#### Email Provider
- Domestic email providers such as QQ Mail and 163 Mail are **not recommended**.
- Major international email services such as Gmail are **recommended**.

## Beginner Recommendation

If you are registering an international platform account for the first time, **start with YouTube**:

1. Register a YouTube account and create a **Gmail address** at the same time.
2. Use this Gmail address to register for other international platforms.
3. This makes account management more consistent and can improve security.

---

**Tip:** Account security is the foundation of long-term operations. Follow platform rules and maintain consistent usage habits.
