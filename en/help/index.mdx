AiToEarn has two main roles: creators complete tasks and earn rewards, while advertisers publish tasks to promote their platforms, products, or content. Choose the role that best matches your goal.

## Quick Start

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Earn as a Creator" icon="badge-dollar-sign" href="./creator">
    For users who want to earn through tasks. Review the requirements, publish or complete the requested action, and receive rewards after completion.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Publish as an Advertiser" icon="megaphone" href="./advertiser">
    For users who want to promote something. Publish a task, set the reward, and ask creators to promote your platform, product, or content.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
