The first step in using Aitoearn is to connect your social media channels. You can then configure your ideal publishing schedule, engage with your audience, analyze results, and **monetize your content**.

Let's get started!

## Connect Your First Channel to Aitoearn

- To speed up the connection process, sign in to your social media channel first, then open Aitoearn in a separate tab.
- After signing in to Aitoearn, click **Add Channels**.
- Select the channel you want to connect.
- When the dialog appears, click **Start Connecting**.
- Authorize Aitoearn to access your social media channel.
- Click **Add to Aitoearn**.
- A confirmation dialog will appear in the upper-right corner when the connection is successful.


## Space Settings

- Every channel authorized in Aitoearn belongs to a space. A space represents an environment, such as a desktop or mobile environment.

- Within a space, you can configure device fingerprint information. The most important device detail is the IP address.

- You can create as many spaces as needed in Aitoearn.

## Create, Schedule, and Publish Content

<a id="compose-schedule-publish"></a>

- Click **+ New Work** in the upper-right corner to open the editor.
    - You can also click the plus icon directly on the calendar.
- Select the channels where you want to publish.
- Write your text, upload images or videos, then select **Customize for each network**. You can now tailor the message for each social media channel.
- Choose to publish immediately, or select **Set date and time** to schedule a custom date and time.
- Finally, click **Publish** to complete the process.
