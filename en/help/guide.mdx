## What are the requirements for creators earning through AiToEarn tasks?

1. Do not delete published content within 30 days. Deleted content will not be settled.

2. The platform settles only authentic, natural, and content-related valid engagement. Meaningless comments, duplicate comments, incentivized spam, and engagement exchanges are not counted.

3. For CPE calculation, a like or share counts as 1 interaction, a save counts as 3, and a comment counts as 5.

## How can I find creators on AiToEarn to promote my product?

1. Aitoearn provides access to a large pool of KOCs and KOLs and supports CPE, CPM, and fixed-price settlement with creators.

2. To help creators accept your task more quickly, we **strongly recommend using one-click publishing**. Create a draft folder in Content Management and use AI or manual creation to prepare publishable drafts. Creators can publish your drafts directly to their social media accounts.

## Will matrix publishing through Aitoearn cause account suspension?

Aitoearn prioritizes account security when designing publishing features. Nearly all publishing APIs use official authorization.

The source code is available on [GitHub](https://github.com/yikart/AiToEarn), where you can review the relevant implementation.

## Important RedNote Reminder

Complete identity verification before using RedNote. RedNote currently applies stricter account controls than most domestic platforms, and identity verification is also recommended on other platforms.

Avoid publishing too much content during the early stage of a new account.

## How can AI automate comments, likes, and other engagement?

Install the Aitoearn browser extension.

Download the latest version from the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/aitoearn-extension/hnmocdhmnfgcjnedmdgfefcobideaeja) or through the Aitoearn website.

> Important: If a RedNote account has low account authority, keep comments below **10 per day** for each account and environment.

The Aitoearn browser extension can automate repetitive browser tasks and schedule recurring daily work.
