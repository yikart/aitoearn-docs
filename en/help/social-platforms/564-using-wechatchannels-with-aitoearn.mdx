## Publishing Requirements

# WeChat Channels Content Specifications

| Category | Image post | Video post |
|--------------|--------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Supported formats** | Images: JPG, JPEG, PNG, BMP | Video: MP4, AVI, MKV, MOV, FLV, RMVB, WMV, WebM |
| **Quantity limit** | Up to 9 images | 1 video per post |
| **Length limit** | Not applicable | Up to 3,600 seconds (60 minutes) per video |
| **File size** | Up to 20MB per image | Up to 1GB per video |
| **Text limit** | Title limit: 16 characters; body limit: 1,000 characters | Title limit: 16 characters; body limit: 1,000 characters |
