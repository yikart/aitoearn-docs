# WeChat Channels Content Specifications

| Category | Image post | Video post |
|--------------|--------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Supported formats** | Images: any format | Video: any format; H.264 and H.265 are recommended for efficient encoding |
| **Quantity limit** | Up to 9 images | 1 video per post |
| **Dimensions/aspect ratio** | Image aspect ratio range: 0.33–3.0 :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1} | Same aspect-ratio range for video, with 16:9 or 9:16 recommended :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}; recommended dimensions: 1080×1260 portrait (6:7) or 1080×608 landscape (16:9) :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3} |
| **Resolution** | — | ≥1080p recommended :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4} |
| **Length limit** | — | Mobile: 3 seconds–30 minutes, usually 30 minutes on older devices :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}; desktop: 3 seconds–2 hours :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6} |
| **File size** | — | Maximum video size: 2GB :contentReference[oaicite:7]{index=7} |
| **Additional notes** | — | GIF images and HDR video uploads are not supported :contentReference[oaicite:8]{index=8}; the platform may compress video, so use standard aspect ratios and encoding to preserve quality :contentReference[oaicite:9]{index=9} |
