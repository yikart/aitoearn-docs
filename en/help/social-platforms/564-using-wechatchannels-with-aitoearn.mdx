## Publishing Requirements

# WeChat Channels Content Specifications

| Category | Video note |
| --- | --- |
| **Supported formats** | Video: MP4, AVI, MKV, MOV, FLV, RMVB, WMV, WebM |
| **Quantity limit** | Up to 1 video per post |
| **Duration limit** | Up to 3,600 seconds (60 minutes) per video |
| **File size** | Up to 1GB per video |
| **Text limit** | Title up to 16 characters; body up to 1,000 characters |
