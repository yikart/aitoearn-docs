## X/Twitter Publishing Requirements

| Content | Text only | Image | Video |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Supported | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |

| Category | Video | Image | GIF |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Allowed per post** | Up to 1 video | Up to 4 images | Up to 4 GIFs, counted toward the image limit |
| **Video length** | Up to 140 seconds (2 minutes 20 seconds) per video | Not applicable | Not applicable |
| **File size** | Up to 512MB per video | Up to 5MB per image | Up to 5MB per GIF |
| **Supported formats** | MP4, MOV | JPG, JPEG, PNG, WebP | GIF |

- Separate titles are not supported.
- Body and total text length are limited to 280 characters per post.
