## Connect and Refresh LinkedIn Pages and Profiles

📝 **Note:** To connect a LinkedIn Page to Aitoearn, you must be a **Super Admin** of that Page. [Learn more about LinkedIn Page administrator roles](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a550647).

Go to your channels area to connect a LinkedIn Page or profile: [https://account.aitoearn.com/channels](https://account.aitoearn.com/channels)

You can connect multiple LinkedIn accounts to Aitoearn. To make sure Aitoearn connects the account you want, sign in to that LinkedIn account in another browser tab before connecting it to Aitoearn.

If a connected LinkedIn channel needs to be refreshed, see Refreshing a channel in Aitoearn.

Here are a few notes for LinkedIn channel types:

| **Account type** | **Aitoearn integration** | **Notes** |
|--------------|------------------|----------|
| **LinkedIn Company Page** | **✓** Authenticated through a LinkedIn profile with Super Admin access. | |
| **LinkedIn Showcase Page** | **✓** Authenticated through a LinkedIn profile with Super Admin access. | |
| **LinkedIn profile** | **✓** Connecting many LinkedIn profiles to Aitoearn should be fine as long as the content shared across profiles is unique. | |
| **LinkedIn Group** | LinkedIn Groups are not available to third parties through the LinkedIn API. | |

**Troubleshooting:** If your LinkedIn Page does not appear when connecting it to Aitoearn, check that:

- You have [Super Admin access](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a541981) to the Page you are trying to connect.
- You are signed in to [LinkedIn.com](http://linkedin.com) with the account that has Super Admin access before connecting the Page to Aitoearn.

**Note:** If you manage multiple LinkedIn Pages across different Aitoearn accounts, it is best to have one Super Admin connect Pages for each Aitoearn account. This helps prevent Page disconnection issues.

## Schedule Posts to LinkedIn Pages and Profiles

LinkedIn Pages and profiles support slightly different features. Use the table below when scheduling posts.

📝 **Note:**

- **Best time to post:** The "best time to post" feature in analytics does not support LinkedIn. To build a better posting schedule, see guidance on the best times to post on LinkedIn.
- **Need content ideas?** See 30 days of LinkedIn post prompts.

| **Feature and notes** | **Details** |
|--------------------|----------|
| **Schedule a first comment** | You can publish the first comment together with a LinkedIn post. The comment comes from the same Page or profile and cannot be delayed or published from another channel. |
| **Schedule documents** | Each post supports 1 PDF document carousel, up to 100MB and 300 pages. A document title is required before publishing or saving. |
| **Tags / mentions** | To mention a LinkedIn Page, paste its LinkedIn link. To mention a profile, type @ and search for the name. LinkedIn Pages can mention only profiles that follow the Page, and profiles can mention only connected profiles. |
| **Hashtags** | Hashtags are not shown as links while editing, but become clickable after publishing to LinkedIn. |
| **Body hyperlinks** | LinkedIn does not support adding hyperlinks to text phrases. Paste the URL directly; it will publish as a clickable link and may generate a link preview. |
| **LinkedIn link previews** | Third-party tools cannot generate preview images for linkedin.com links. Upload an image manually if you need one. |
| **Audience scope** | Due to API limitations, "connections only" audience settings are not currently supported. Use native LinkedIn publishing if you need to limit the audience. |
| **Daily posting limit** | Up to 50 posts every 24 hours. |
| **Character limit** | Up to 3,000 characters per post and 1,248 characters for the first comment. |
| **Post attribution** | The publisher shown at the top of a LinkedIn Page post is the LinkedIn administrator who connected the Page to Aitoearn. Followers cannot see it. |
| **Not supported** | LinkedIn polls and Life tab publishing are not currently supported. |

## LinkedIn Image, Video, GIF, and Document (PDF) Specifications

LinkedIn supports images, videos, GIFs, and PDF documents.

| Category | Video | Image | GIF | Document (PDF carousel) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Allowed per post** | Up to 1 | Up to 20 images | Up to 1 | Up to 1 |
| **Video length** | 3 seconds to 30 minutes | Not applicable | 3-60 seconds | Not applicable |
| **File size** | Up to 1GB | Up to 10MB | Up to 10MB | Up to 100MB, up to 300 pages |
| **Dimensions / aspect ratio** | 1280 x 720 px | 1200 x 627 px, or square image | Not available | Not available |
| **Supported formats** | MP4, MOV, AVI, M4V, or video link | JPG, PNG, HEIC, WebP | GIF | PDF |
| **Custom thumbnail** | Not supported for uploaded videos; supported for video links | Not applicable | Not supported | Not applicable |
| **Autoplay** | Supported for MP4 files from 75KB to 200MB | Not applicable | Supported | Not applicable |
| **Alt text** | Not applicable | Supported | Not applicable | Not applicable |

- If a GIF fails with a generic error, it may have too many frames. Try shortening the GIF, compressing it, or converting it to a video.
- When adding a document (PDF carousel), add a document title. Otherwise, the post cannot be scheduled.
- Actual publishing depends on Aitoearn page validation.

## LinkedIn Analytics

Publishing is only one part of the process. Analytics help you understand whether your strategy resonates with followers.

In Aitoearn, LinkedIn analytics are available in two places: the publishing dashboard under the **Sent** tab and the **Analyze** tab.

### Sent Post Analytics

In the **Sent** tab, you can quickly view metrics for sent posts, including LinkedIn Pages and profiles.

**LinkedIn profile analytics include:**

- Total impressions
- Likes
- Comments
- Shares
- Engagement rate
- Views
- Watch time
- Unique viewers for profile video posts

**LinkedIn Page analytics include:**

- On the free plan, you can access likes, comments, and impressions. Paid plans also include likes, comments, impressions, shares, and engagement rate.

### Analytics

If you subscribe to an Essentials or Team plan, you can use the **Analyze** tab at the top of the dashboard to view advanced analytics for **LinkedIn Pages**. Advanced analytics for LinkedIn profiles are not currently supported.

To view advanced analytics for a LinkedIn Page, select **LinkedIn** on the left and review these tabs:

- **Overview:** Shows overall engagement over a selected period.
- **Posts:** Shows the history and performance of published posts.
- **Answers:** Answers questions such as the best date, content type, and publishing frequency.

### 📝 Notes

- You will see analytics for posts created natively on LinkedIn, but analytics for PDF carousel posts are not available.
- When you first connect a LinkedIn Page, Aitoearn backfills up to 6 months or 500 posts of data.
- Analytics refresh daily for the 500 most recent LinkedIn Page posts. Once a post is no longer among the 500 most recent posts, its data stops refreshing.
- LinkedIn sponsored post metrics can be retrieved.

## Best Practices to Avoid Suspension When Sharing to Multiple Profiles

Although many LinkedIn profiles can technically be connected to Aitoearn, this is not recommended and may lead to account suspension on LinkedIn.

Social networks, especially LinkedIn and Facebook, increasingly restrict the use of personal profiles for business and advertising. They expect profiles to represent individual people and share authentic content created by the profile owner.

If LinkedIn detects duplicate content shared across profiles, it may suspend those accounts. Suspensions are usually temporary, but repeated behavior can lead to repeated suspensions.
