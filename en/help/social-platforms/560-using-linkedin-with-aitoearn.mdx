## Connect and Refresh LinkedIn Pages and Profiles

📝 **Note:** To connect a LinkedIn Page to Aitoearn, you must be a **Super Admin** of that Page. [Learn more about LinkedIn Page administrator roles](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a550647).

Go to the channel area to connect a LinkedIn Page or profile: [Open the channel area](https://账户.aitoearn.com/channels)

You can connect multiple LinkedIn accounts to Aitoearn. To ensure that Aitoearn connects the correct account, sign in to that LinkedIn account in another browser tab before starting the connection.

If a connected LinkedIn channel needs to be refreshed, see [Refreshing a channel in Aitoearn](https://aitoearn.helpscoutdocs.com/article/573-refreshing-a-channel-在-aitoearn).

Consider the following details for each LinkedIn channel type:

| **Account type** | **Aitoearn integration** | **Notes** |
|--------------|------------------|----------|
| **LinkedIn Company Page** | **✓** Authenticated through a LinkedIn profile with Super Admin access. | |
| **LinkedIn Showcase Page** | **✓** Authenticated through a LinkedIn profile with Super Admin access. | |
| **LinkedIn profile** | **✓** Connecting many LinkedIn profiles to Aitoearn should not cause issues as long as the content shared across profiles is unique. | |
| **LinkedIn Group** | LinkedIn Groups are not available to third parties through the LinkedIn API. | |

**Troubleshooting:** If your LinkedIn Page does not appear when connecting it to Aitoearn, verify that:

- You have [Super Admin access](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a541981) to the Page.
- You are signed in to [LinkedIn.com](http://linkedin.com) with the account that has Super Admin access before connecting the Page to Aitoearn.

**Note:** If you manage multiple LinkedIn Pages across different Aitoearn accounts, use a separate Super Admin to connect Pages for each Aitoearn account when possible. This can help prevent disconnections.

LinkedIn supports images, videos, and GIFs.

| Category | Video | Image | GIF |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Allowed per post** | Up to 1 video | 2 to 20 images | 2 to 20 GIFs, counted toward the image limit |

## LinkedIn Analytics

Publishing is only part of a social strategy. Analytics show whether your content resonates with followers.

LinkedIn analytics are available in two places in Aitoearn: the publishing dashboard under the **Sent** tab and the **Analyze** tab.

### Sent Post Analytics

The **Sent** tab provides a quick view of metrics for posts sent to LinkedIn Pages and profiles.

**LinkedIn profile analytics include:**

- Total impressions
- Likes
- Comments
- Shares
- Engagement rate
- Views
- Watch time
- Unique viewers for profile video posts

**LinkedIn Page analytics include:**

- The free plan includes likes, comments, and impressions. [Paid plans](http://aitoearn.com/定价) also include shares and engagement rate. ![LinkedIn sent posts](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/64cb6cf86d4a3e191f237e66/图片s/66b67e3c48915f570c6a93ee/文件-KM1AzWd81N.jpg)

## Best Practices to Avoid Suspension When Sharing to Multiple Profiles

Although many LinkedIn profiles can technically be connected to Aitoearn, doing so is not recommended and may result in account suspension by LinkedIn.

Social networks, particularly LinkedIn and Facebook, increasingly restrict the use of personal profiles for business and advertising. They expect each profile to represent an individual and share authentic content created by that profile owner.

If LinkedIn identifies duplicate content shared across profiles, it may suspend those accounts. Suspensions are often temporary, but repeated behavior can lead to repeated suspensions.
