> Note: YouTube support is currently in beta. Features and permissions may change.

## Connection Requirements
- Sign in and connect using the Google account that owns the YouTube channel. Brand Account managers are not currently supported.
- Complete YouTube email verification. Unverified accounts can publish up to 10 posts within 24 hours and cannot add external links to descriptions. These restrictions do not apply after verification.


## Publishing Requirements
| Content | Text only | Image | Video |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Supported | x | x | ✓ |

- Up to 1 video per post.
- Title limit: 100 characters. Description limit: 5,000 characters.
- Maximum video size: 256GB.
- Maximum video length: 43,200 seconds (12 hours).
- Supported formats: MP4, MOV, AVI, WMV, FLV, and MKV.
