## Publishing Requirements

| Content | Text only | Image | Video |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Supported | ✖️ | ✓ | ✓ |

| Category | Image post | Video post |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Supported formats** | Images: JPG, JPEG, PNG, and other common formats | Video: MP4 and MOV (H.264 recommended); WebM is supported |
| **Quantity limit** | Up to 9 images | 1 video per post |
| **Dimensions** | 1080×1920px recommended (portrait preferred)<br />Supports aspect ratios from 4:3 to 3:4, such as 1080×810–1080×1440px | Portrait 9:16 (1080×1920px) recommended; landscape 16:9 and square 1:1 are also supported |
| **Resolution** | ≥720p recommended for clear image quality | Up to 1080p; higher resolutions are compressed |
| **Length limit** | N/A | Standard users: ≤15 minutes<br />Some creators can upload videos longer than 10 minutes |
| **File size** | ≤5MB per image recommended; larger files may affect loading and distribution | ≤1GB per video; ≤300MB recommended to reduce upload failures |
| **Frame rate and audio** | N/A | 30 FPS recommended, AAC audio at ≥128kbps |
| **Cover image** | The first image is used as the cover; 1080×1440px portrait recommended | Custom covers supported; 1080×1440px portrait or 1440×1080px landscape recommended |
| **Text** | Title + post body; 300–800 Chinese characters recommended, with images to improve distribution | Keep video descriptions concise and include tags/topics; avoid overly long text |
