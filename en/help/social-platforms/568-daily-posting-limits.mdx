Each social network has its own guidance on how much content can be published each day.  
For example, Facebook recommends publishing up to 5 posts per day, but the actual allowed limit is 35. Exceeding this may affect post reach.  

Based on official platform rules, we summarized the daily posting limits below.  
These limits **do not** affect how many posts you can store in your queue.

| Social platform | Posts allowed every 24 hours |
|----------------|----------------------|
| Facebook | 35 |
| Instagram | 50* |
| X/Twitter | 100 |
| LinkedIn | 50 |
| Pinterest | Verified email users: 25<br />Unverified email users: 5 |
| TikTok | 25 |
| YouTube Shorts | Unverified users: 10<br />Verified users: unlimited |
| Threads | 250 |
