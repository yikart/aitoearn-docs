title: "Daily Posting Limits"
description: ""
---
Each social network has its own guidelines for how much content you can publish each day.

For example, Facebook recommends publishing no more than 5 posts per day, although the actual limit is 35. Publishing more may affect post reach.

The table below summarizes the daily posting limits defined by each social platform.

These limits **do not** affect the number of posts you can store in your queue.

| Social platform | Posts per 24 hours |
|----------------|----------------------|
| Facebook       | 35                   |
| Instagram      | 50*                  |
| X/Twitter      | 100                  |
| LinkedIn       | 50                   |
| Pinterest      | Verified email: 25<br />Unverified email: 5 |
| TikTok         | 25                   |
| YouTube Shorts | Unverified users: 10<br />Verified users: Unlimited |
| Threads        | 250                  |
