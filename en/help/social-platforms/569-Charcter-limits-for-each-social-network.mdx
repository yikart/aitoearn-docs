When scheduling posts, note that each social network has its own character limits.

The table below lists the maximum character count for each platform.

| Platform/channel | Character limit |
|-------------------------|----------|
| Facebook Pages | Body: 63,206 characters |
| Instagram accounts | Body: 2,200 characters |
| X/Twitter profiles | Body and total text: 280 characters |
| LinkedIn Pages and profiles | Title: 200 characters<br />Body: 3,000 characters |
| Pinterest boards | Title: 100 characters<br />Body: 800 characters |
| TikTok | Body and total text: 2,200 characters |
| YouTube | Title: 100 characters<br />Body: 5,000 characters |
| Threads | Body and total text: 500 characters |
