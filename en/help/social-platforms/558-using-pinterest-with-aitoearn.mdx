## Features and Good-to-Knows

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Schedule posts | Supports scheduling Pinterest Pins. |
| Tags / mentions | Mentioning or tagging Pinterest profiles in scheduled Pins is not currently supported. Tagging topics on Pinterest is also not supported. |
| Hashtags | Pinterest does not support clickable hashtag links, so hashtags published to Pinterest are not clickable. |
| Daily posting limit | Verified email users can publish up to 25 posts every 24 hours. Unverified email users can publish up to 5 posts every 24 hours. |
| Character limit | Each Pin can contain up to 500 characters. |

## Pinterest Image, Video, and GIF Specifications

| Item | Video | Image | GIF |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Allowed per post | Up to 1 | Up to 1 image | Up to 1 GIF |
| Video length | 4 seconds to 15 minutes | Not applicable | Not applicable |
| File size | Up to 1GB | Up to 10MB | Up to 3MB |
| Dimensions / aspect ratio | Recommended 1:1, 2:3, 4:5, or 9:16 | Not applicable | Not applicable |
| Supported formats | MP4, MOV, AVI, M4V | JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC | GIF |
| Custom thumbnail | Supported | Not applicable | Not applicable |
| Autoplay | Supported | Not applicable | Supported |
| Canva | Supported | Supported | Not applicable |
| Giphy | Not applicable | Not applicable | Supported |
| Alt text | Not applicable | Supported | Not applicable |
