## How Instagram Works with Aitoearn

Instagram works a little differently from other platforms. To publish automatically, you need to meet these requirements:
- [Use an Instagram Business or Creator account](#switch-to-professional).
- [Make sure the content meets Instagram requirements](#media-specs), and belongs to one of these types:
   - Single or multiple images (carousel)
   - Single video post
   - Reels (vertical videos up to 90 seconds)
   - Stories (vertical images or videos up to 15 seconds)

> Note: Due to API limitations, Creator accounts do not currently support automatic Stories publishing.

## Connect and Refresh an Instagram Account

1. Sign in to the target Instagram account in your browser.
2. Return to Aitoearn and connect the Instagram account in "Space".
3. You can connect multiple Instagram accounts to Aitoearn and refresh connections when needed.

## Find Your Instagram Account Type

Follow these steps to confirm whether your account is personal, business, or creator:
1. Open Instagram on your phone and go to your profile.
2. Tap the menu icon in the upper-right corner.
3. Check the prompt near the bottom:
   - "Business tools and controls" means it is a Business account.
   - "Account type and tools" means it is a Personal account.
   - "Creator tools and controls" means it is a Creator account.

## Switch Instagram to a Professional Account (Creator or Business)

1. Open Instagram on your phone and tap the menu in the upper-right corner.
2. Scroll to "For professionals" and open "Account type and tools".
3. Tap "Switch to professional account". If you see "Business tools and controls" instead, the account is already professional. You can return to Aitoearn to reconnect or refresh the channel.
4. Follow the prompts to the category page and choose a suitable category.
5. Select "Creator" or "Business". The account will then be a professional account.

## Plan and Publish (Posts / Reels / Stories)

- Supports publishing and scheduling from web and mobile app.
- To mention with @ in captions, enter the exact username. Search while typing is not currently supported. Mentions become clickable after publishing.
- Tagging users in images is supported only for automatically published image posts. Reels, Stories, and notification publishing do not support image tags.
- Each post can include up to 20 @ mentions.
- Shop Grid: You can add external links to posts and create a clickable grid page from the bio link. Personal accounts are not supported.

### Features and Good-to-Knows

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Daily posting limit | Up to 50 posts every 24 hours, including posts, Stories, and Reels. |
| Character limit | Up to 2,200 characters per post; up to 2,196 characters for the first comment; up to 120 characters for Stories published by notification. |
| Alt text | Supported only for image posts, including single-image posts and carousels. Videos, Reels, and Stories are not currently supported. |
| Reposting | Scheduled reposts are not currently supported. |
| Location | You can add locations to posts and Reels. Stories are not supported. |
| Hashtags | Enter hashtags in the editor. They will appear as clickable links after publishing. |

### Common Limits and Notes

- Due to API limitations, trending music tracks cannot currently be added to posts or Reels.
- Creator accounts cannot automatically publish Stories.
- When sharing Instagram links to other social networks, link previews are not guaranteed.
- If Instagram is set to "auto-share to Facebook", content sent through Aitoearn will not automatically sync to Facebook.
- If you need to schedule multiple videos or images for Stories, create each asset as a separate Story.

## How to Add a Video Cover

After upload:
1. Click "Edit thumbnail".
2. Drag the slider to the frame you want to use as the cover.
3. Click "Save".

## Engage with Instagram Posts

- View and reply to comments in Aitoearn's Engage area.
- Filter by sentiment or tag to improve efficiency.

## Instagram Analytics

If you use an Essentials or Team plan, you can view detailed data in Analyze:
- Overview: View aggregated metrics such as engagement and reach over a selected period.
- Posts: Browse historical performance and details for published content.
- Insights: Answer common strategy questions such as "when to post" and "what to post".

Prerequisite: Instagram must be a professional account and correctly connected to a Facebook Page.

Historical data: When first connected, Aitoearn can backfill up to the past 30 days of data, excluding Stories.

Follower threshold: Due to API limitations, at least 100 followers are required to retrieve analytics data. Accounts with fewer than 100 followers can still connect, but analytics data will not be available.

For more metric details, see Instagram Metric Descriptions.

## Instagram Error Library

When using a scheduling tool like Aitoearn to publish Instagram posts, the social network may sometimes reject a post and return an error. Find the matching error in the table below and follow the solution.

| Error | Cause / Solution |
|------|----------------|
| **"It looks like Aitoearn has lost authorization to post on behalf of this account. Please reauthorize and try again. If the issue persists, contact us. Here are the steps to refresh the connection."** | The full permissions were not granted when the Instagram account was added. Reauthorize the Aitoearn connection with Facebook/Instagram. In Facebook **Edit settings**, select all Pages and permissions, then retry. |
| **"Oops! The attached video is in a format Instagram does not support. Please make sure the video is .MP4 or .MOV and has a bitrate below 5 MBps. Reupload the video and try again."** | The video format or settings are not supported. Check and adjust the video to match Instagram requirements. If the specs are correct, try restoring the default thumbnail and uploading again. |
| **"Oops! Your account is not correctly set up as a Business or Creator account. Please make sure the account is configured for automatic publishing and review our guide."** | Personal Instagram accounts do not support automatic publishing. Switch the account to a **Business** or **Creator** account. |
| **"Uh oh! Your account may be temporarily restricted. Please log in to the Instagram app directly, add your date of birth, then return to Aitoearn and refresh the channel."** | The account may be restricted due to Instagram community guidelines or terms. Update the profile directly in the Instagram app, then refresh the channel in Aitoearn. |
| **"Oops! Something went wrong while publishing. Please edit the post, change the caption, rename the video file, and create a new post."** | Instagram spam detection may have been triggered. Change the content, copy the text into a new post, rename the media file, and try again. You can also use common hashtags, move links from the caption to the bio, and rename the video file, such as `Video1.mp4`. |
| **"It looks like Aitoearn has lost authorization to post on your behalf. Please follow these steps to reauthorize Aitoearn."** | Similar to the first error. The access token has expired. Reauthorize and make sure all Pages and permissions are selected in Facebook Edit settings. |
| **"Oops! Instagram is restricting this post. Please edit the post and try again."** | Instagram may identify the content as spam. If the issue continues, contact Instagram support directly. |
| **"Oops! This image cannot be shared to Instagram because one of the tagged accounts is private or does not exist. Please check that the username is correct and public."** | The post tagged a private or invalid account. Confirm the tagged account is valid and public. |
| **"Sorry! This post hit a known Instagram API error. Trying again later may help."** | Instagram API bug. Wait and try again later. |
| **"This channel has reached the daily posting limit. Please reduce scheduled posts and try again later."** | Instagram's 24-hour limit has been reached. Try again the next day. |
| **"Oops! The attached media file is too large or the video is too long. Remove the media and upload a smaller file."** | The file exceeds the limit, such as images over 8MB. Compress or resize the file and upload again. |
| **"Oops! We encountered a permission issue while posting on your behalf. Please reauthorize the Aitoearn app and grant all Page permissions in Facebook Edit settings."** | Permissions are incomplete. Reauthorize and confirm all required permissions are enabled. |
| **"Oops! Aitoearn does not have permission to post on your behalf. Try refreshing this account in settings."** | Similar authorization issue. Reauthorize the connection. |
| **"Oops! Please edit the post, change the copy, rename the video file, and create a new post."** | Instagram spam detection may have been triggered. Modify the content and upload again. |
| **"Error: Timeout while downloading media. Please create a new post and reupload the media."** | Media download timeout, such as Facebook error `-2`. Create a new post and upload the media again. |
| **"Error: Instagram restricted this post. Please edit the content and try again."** | Instagram or the API temporarily restricted the post. Possible causes include too many hashtags, too many @ mentions, duplicate hashtags, invalid location use, or links in the caption. Create a new post, reduce hashtags and links, and try again after 24 hours. |
| **"Oops! No Instagram account was found for this Facebook account."** | The Facebook account does not have admin access to the related Page, so Aitoearn cannot identify Instagram. Confirm you are a Page admin. |
| **"You are using an Instagram Creator account. Only Business accounts support automatic Instagram Stories publishing."** | Only **Business accounts** support automatic Stories scheduling. Creator accounts can use Aitoearn's Notify Me flow. |
| **"Oops! Facebook requires additional permissions to continue publishing. Please complete Page Publishing Authorization."** | Facebook may require identity verification for high-impact Pages. Follow the prompt and refresh the channel in Aitoearn. |
| **"It looks like Aitoearn has lost authorization to post on behalf of this social account. Please reauthorize and try again. If the issue persists, contact us."** | Same as the authorization issues above. Reauthorization is required. |
| **"Oops! We encountered a known Instagram bug while sending the post. Please try these troubleshooting steps."** | Known Instagram issue. One workaround is to switch the personal account back to a professional account and reconnect. |
| **"We encountered a problem. Please try again. If you are connecting a brand-new Instagram Business account, Meta setup may not be complete yet, such as following accounts, adding an avatar, and filling out the profile."** | New Business accounts must complete basic Meta setup before they can connect to Aitoearn. |
| **"Seeing a different error? Contact us at hello@aitoearn.com with the error message and we will help."** | If you see an error not listed here, contact Aitoearn support. |
