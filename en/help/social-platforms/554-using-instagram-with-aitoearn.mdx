## How Instagram Works with Aitoearn

Instagram works slightly differently from other platforms. Automatic publishing requires:
- [An Instagram business or creator account](#switch-to-professional).
- [Content that meets Instagram requirements](#media-specs) and uses one of these formats:
   - One or more images (carousel)
   - A single video post
   - Reels (portrait videos up to 90 seconds)
   - Stories (portrait images or videos up to 15 seconds)

> Note: Due to API limitations, creator accounts cannot currently publish Stories automatically.

## Connect and Refresh an Instagram Account

1. Sign in to the target Instagram account in your browser.
2. Return to Aitoearn and connect the Instagram account from **Spaces**.
3. You can connect multiple Instagram accounts to Aitoearn and refresh their connections when needed.

## Find Your Instagram Account Type

Follow these steps to check whether you have a personal, business, or creator account:
1. Open Instagram on your phone and go to your profile.
2. Tap the menu icon in the upper-right corner.
3. Check the option shown near the bottom of the page:
   - **Business tools and controls** indicates a business account.
   - **Account type and tools** indicates a personal account.
   - **Creator tools and controls** indicates a creator account.

<a id="switch-to-professional"></a>
## Switch an Instagram Account to a Professional Account

1. Open Instagram on your phone and tap the menu in the upper-right corner.
2. Scroll to **For professionals**, then open **Account type and tools**.
3. Tap **Switch to professional account**. If you see **Business tools and controls** instead, the account is already professional. Return to Aitoearn and reconnect or refresh the channel.
4. Continue to the category page and select an appropriate category.
5. Select **Creator** or **Business**. The account is now a professional account.

## Plan and Publish Posts, Reels, and Stories

- Publishing and scheduling are supported on the web and mobile app.
- To mention an account in a caption, enter its exact username after `@`. Search while typing is not currently supported. The mention becomes clickable after publishing.
- Tagging users in images is supported only for automatically published image posts. It is not supported for Reels, Stories, or notification publishing.
- Each post can contain up to 20 mentions.
- Shop Grid lets you add an external link to each post and create a clickable grid page from the link in your bio. Personal accounts are not supported.

### Frequency and Limits

- Up to 50 posts, Stories, and Reels every 24 hours.
- Caption limit: 2,200 characters. First-comment limit: 2,196 characters.
- Alt text is currently supported only for single-image and carousel posts. It is not supported for videos, Reels, or Stories.
- Instagram repost scheduling is not currently supported.
- Locations can be added to posts and Reels, but not Stories.
- Enter hashtags in the editor. They become clickable links after publishing.

### Common Limitations

- Popular music tracks cannot currently be added to posts or Reels due to API limitations.
- Creator accounts cannot publish Stories automatically.
- Instagram links shared to other social networks may not generate a link preview.
- If Instagram is configured to automatically share to Facebook, content sent through Aitoearn will not be automatically cross-posted to Facebook.
- To schedule multiple images or videos as Stories, create a separate Story for each media item.

## Add a Cover to a Video

After the video finishes uploading:
1. Click **Edit thumbnail**.
2. Drag the slider to the frame you want to use as the cover.
3. Click **Save**.

## Engage with Instagram Posts

- View and reply to comments from **Engage** in Aitoearn.
- Filter by sentiment or label to process comments more efficiently.

## Instagram Analytics

If you use the Essentials or Team plan, detailed analytics are available in **Analyze**:
- Overview: View core metrics such as engagement and reach over a selected period.
- Posts: Review the history and detailed performance of published content.
- Insights: Answer common strategy questions such as when to publish and what content to create.

Requirement: The Instagram account must be professional and correctly connected to a Facebook Page.

Historical data: Aitoearn can retrieve up to 30 days of data after the first connection, excluding Stories.

Follower threshold: Due to API limitations, the account must have at least 100 followers to retrieve analytics. Accounts with fewer than 100 followers can still connect but will not display analytics.

See the Instagram metrics guide for more details.

## Instagram Error Library

Instagram may occasionally reject a post sent through a scheduling tool such as Aitoearn and return an error. Find the matching message below and follow the suggested solution.

| Error | Cause / solution |
|------|----------------|
| **"It looks like Aitoearn has lost authorization to post on behalf of this account. Please reauthorize and try again."** | Full permissions were not granted when the Instagram account was added. Reauthorize the Aitoearn connection to Facebook and Instagram. In Facebook **Edit settings**, select every required Page and permission, then try publishing again. |
| **"The attached video is in a format Instagram does not support. Make sure it is an MP4 or MOV with a bitrate below 5 MBps."** | The video format or specifications are unsupported. Adjust the video to meet Instagram requirements. If the specifications are correct, restore the default thumbnail and upload it again. |
| **"Your account is not correctly configured as a business or creator account."** | Personal Instagram accounts do not support automatic publishing. Switch the account to a **Business** or **Creator** account. |
| **"Your account may be temporarily restricted. Sign in to Instagram and add your date of birth."** | The account may be restricted under Instagram's Community Guidelines or Terms. Update the account in the Instagram app, then return to Aitoearn and refresh the channel. |
| **"There was a problem publishing the post. Edit the caption, rename the video file, and create a new post."** | Instagram's spam detection was triggered. Change the post content, create a new post, rename the media file, and publish again. You can also use common hashtags, move caption links to the bio, and rename video files, such as `Video1.mp4`. |
| **"Aitoearn has lost authorization to post on your behalf. Please reauthorize Aitoearn."** | The access token has expired. Reauthorize the account and select every required Page and permission in Facebook Edit settings. |
| **"Instagram is restricting this post. Edit the content and try again."** | Instagram may consider the content spam. If the issue continues, contact Instagram support. |
| **"This image cannot be shared because a tagged account is private or does not exist."** | The post tags a private or invalid account. Confirm that every tagged account exists and is public. |
| **"This post encountered a known Instagram API error."** | This is a known Instagram API issue. Wait and try again later. |
| **"This channel has reached its daily publishing limit."** | Instagram has reached its daily limit. Wait until the next day and try again. |
| **"The attached media is too large or the video is too long."** | The file exceeds Instagram limits, such as an image larger than 8MB. Compress or resize the file and upload it again. |
| **"There is a problem with the permission to post on your behalf."** | Permissions are missing or incomplete. Reauthorize Aitoearn and grant access to every required Page. |
| **"Aitoearn does not have permission to post on your behalf."** | Refresh the account connection in settings and complete authorization again. |
| **"Edit the post, change the copy, rename the video file, and create a new post."** | Instagram's spam detection was triggered. Modify the content and upload it again as a new post. |
| **"Error: Timed out while downloading media."** | The media download timed out and Facebook returned error `-2`. Create a new post and upload the media again. |
| **"Instagram restricted this post from publishing."** | Instagram or its API temporarily restricted the post. Possible causes include too many hashtags or mentions, duplicate hashtags, incorrect location usage, or links in the caption. Create a new post, reduce hashtags and links, wait 24 hours, and try again. |
| **"No Instagram account associated with this Facebook account was found."** | The Facebook account does not have administrator access to the corresponding Page. Confirm that you are a Page administrator. |
| **"You are using an Instagram creator account. Only business accounts support automatic Instagram Stories."** | Only **Business** accounts support automatic Story scheduling. Creator accounts can use Aitoearn's **Notify Me** feature. |
| **"Facebook requires additional permissions to continue publishing."** | High-reach Facebook Pages may require identity or Page Publishing Authorization. Complete the requested security checks and refresh the channel in Aitoearn. |
| **"Aitoearn has lost authorization to post for this social account."** | Reauthorize the account and try again. |
| **"A known Instagram bug occurred while sending the post."** | One possible workaround is to switch the personal account back to a professional account and reconnect it. |
| **"We encountered a problem. If this is a new Instagram business account, Meta setup may not be complete."** | Complete the basic Meta account setup, such as following accounts, adding a profile image, and completing the bio, before connecting to Aitoearn. |
| **"Seeing a different error? Contact hello@aitoearn.com and include the error message."** | Contact the Aitoearn support team for errors not listed above. |
