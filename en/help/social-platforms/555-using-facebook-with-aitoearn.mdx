## Facebook Features Available through Aitoearn

Available features differ between Facebook Pages and Groups due to API limitations. Refer to the table below when scheduling content.

| **Feature and notes** | **Details** |
| --- | --- |
| Publishing | User mentions are not supported; polls are not supported; Stories support only 1 image or 1 video |
| User mentions | Coming soon |
| Daily posting limit | 25 posts every 24 hours |
| Character limit | 5,000 characters per post or Reel |
| Hashtags | Enter hashtags for posts or Reels in the editor. Stories do not currently support hashtags. Hashtags become clickable after publishing. |

> Note: The **Best time to post** analytics feature does not currently support Facebook.

Facebook supports images, videos, and GIFs. Reel scheduling is available only for Facebook Pages.

| Category | Video | Image | GIF |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Allowed per post** | Up to 1 video | Up to 10 images | Up to 10 GIFs, counted toward the image limit |
| **Video length** | Up to 90 seconds (1 minute 30 seconds) per video | Not applicable | Not applicable |
| **File size** | Up to 1GB per video | Up to 10MB per image | Up to 10MB per GIF |
| **Supported formats** | MP4, MOV, AVI | JPG, JPEG, PNG, BMP, TIFF | GIF |

- Up to 63,206 characters per post.

## Important Considerations

To protect your Facebook account and avoid spam flags, follow these recommendations:

When identical content is shared to multiple Facebook Pages at the same time, Facebook may identify the posts as spam or automated behavior. Posts may fail, the access token may be revoked, and the account may need to be refreshed in Aitoearn. In some cases, Facebook may temporarily restrict the account.

Stagger the publishing time for each Page, for example:
- Page 1: 09:30
- Page 2: 09:34
- Page 3: 09:39
- Page 4: 09:42
- Page 5: 09:47

# Facebook Error Library

Facebook may occasionally reject a post sent through a scheduling tool such as Aitoearn and return an error. Find the matching error below and follow the suggested solution.

| Error | Cause | Solution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "The daily posting limit for this channel has been reached" | The daily publishing limit has been reached | Reduce the number of posts scheduled for the day and try again later. |
| "Aitoearn lost authorization to post on your behalf" | The Facebook password was recently changed, the token expired, or permissions are incomplete | Reauthorize the connection. In Facebook **Edit settings**, select every required Page and permission, return to **Manage channels** in Aitoearn, refresh the connection, and try again. |
| "Please ensure you're an Admin for the FB Group and Aitoearn has been added as an app" | The authorized profile is not a Group administrator or does not have sufficient permissions | Confirm that the profile is still a Group administrator, add the Aitoearn app in the Group settings, refresh the connection in **Manage channels**, and try again. |
| "Please ensure you're a Page administrator and Two-Factor Authentication is enabled" | The authorized account lacks administrator access or has not enabled 2FA | Confirm Page administrator access and enable 2FA in Facebook, then refresh the connection and try again. |
| "Trouble uploading media/image" | An unknown upload error occurred, or the image does not meet format or size requirements | Retry the post and confirm that the image is JPG, PNG, or HEIC and no larger than 4MB. |
| "We're investigating a potential Facebook bug; the post may already be published" | A Facebook publishing issue occurred, but the post may be live | Check Facebook.com first. If the post is live, remove the failed item from the queue. Use native publishing temporarily if needed. |
| "Publishing Authorization is required" | Facebook requires publishing authorization for a high-reach Page | Complete Facebook Publishing Authorization, enable 2FA, and confirm that location services are enabled. Then refresh the channel. |
| "Your content couldn't be shared because it goes against our Community Standards" | The link was reported or identified as violating Facebook rules | Try publishing the same content directly on Facebook. If it still fails, follow Facebook's instructions to appeal. |
| "Please log in to facebook.com and follow the prompts" | The account requires a temporary security check | Sign in to Facebook and complete the prompts, then refresh the channel and try again. |
| "We're bumping into a few Facebook Page limits" | The Page has low overall engagement or triggered a rate limit within 24 hours | Temporarily reduce publishing frequency, such as one post every 24 hours, and resume scheduling after engagement improves. |
| "An unknown error has occurred" | A temporary unknown error occurred | Try again later. Contact support if the error continues. |
| "Aitoearn doesn't have permission to post on your behalf" | Required permissions were not fully selected during connection | Reauthorize the account, select every required Page and permission, refresh the connection, and try again. |

> Tip: When reauthorizing, sign in with a Facebook profile that has administrator access and select every required Page, Group, and permission during authorization.
